# Contact — Surface Brief

## Scope and mode

- **Surface:** Contact page
- **Mode:** Persuade
- **Build path:** Code-led

## Audience

Visitor ready to reach out. Came from a case study or homepage. Already convinced or nearly convinced. Needs zero friction between intent and action.

## Job

The visitor sends an email or connects on LinkedIn within 30 seconds of landing. Primary action: click mailto link or LinkedIn profile link.

## Proof and content

- Email address: ajujosephj09@gmail.com (mailto link)
- LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/aju-joseph-john/
- Substack: https://ajujosephjohn.substack.com/
- GitHub: https://github.com/ajujosephj09-hungryexplorer
- One invitation line (concise, not clever)

## Content blocks

1. Nav: name (left), page links, social icons (same as landing page)
2. Invitation line: one sentence
3. Primary contact: email (mailto link), LinkedIn (profile link), prominent
4. Secondary links: Substack, GitHub
5. Footer: social links, minimal (same as landing page)

## Anti-goals

No contact form in V1. No calendar booking widget. No map. No "let's build something together" copy. No decorative content padding the page.

## Constraints

- WCAG 2.1 AA
- Sub 2 second LCP
- All copy through Humanizer Gate then Post Humanizer Gate
- Contact method: mailto for V1 (form service like Formspree is TBD, per PRD)
- Mobile: contact links tappable, generous hit targets
