# About — Surface Brief

## Scope and mode

- **Surface:** About page
- **Mode:** Read
- **Build path:** Code-led

## Audience

Hiring manager who already read a case study or scanned the homepage. Wants the full picture: is the breadth deliberate or scattered? Does the career trajectory make sense? May also be a PE/VC search firm confirming career scope before a first meeting.

## Job

After reading, the visitor says "The career trajectory makes sense. The range is deliberate, not random." Primary action: read a case study they have not seen yet, or contact.

## Proof and content

- Career narrative: 3-4 thematic paragraphs organized around the positioning line ("built products from zero and run the P&L to scale them"), not chronological
- Experience timeline: period, role, company, 1-2 proof points per entry
- Mapleview Partners shown as continuous advisory practice (started 2023, paused for CanadaHelps Sep 2023, resumed Jul 2026)
- CanadaHelps framed with commercial scale in every mention
- Career gap (Nov 2021 to Aug 2023) framed as deliberate relocation + productive consulting
- Education: Duke MBA, B.Tech (First Class with Distinction), Pragmatic Institute Certified
- What drives Aju, what he avoids (sourced from mnookin two-pager)

## Content blocks

1. Nav: name (left), page links, social icons (same as landing page)
2. Career narrative: 3-4 thematic paragraphs, positioning line as organizing thread
3. Experience timeline: ruled vertical list, each entry with period, role, company, 1-2 proof points
4. Education: Duke MBA, B.Tech, Pragmatic Institute
5. What drives me: closing section sourced from mnookin two-pager
6. CTA/Contact: email, LinkedIn, invitation line
7. Footer: social links, minimal (same as landing page)

## Anti-goals

No resume rehash. No skills grid or tag cloud. No horizontal timeline. No headshot. No "fun facts" section. No testimonial placeholders (none exist; the site must not fabricate any).

## Constraints

- WCAG 2.1 AA
- Sub 2 second LCP
- Person schema markup
- CanadaHelps named and framed with commercial scale in every mention (ADR 0008)
- Every claim supported by source documents (ADR 0001)
- All copy through Humanizer Gate then Post Humanizer Gate
- Mobile: timeline entries stack vertically, narrative readable at mobile widths
