# ADR 0007: Homepage Takeaway Line

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-10-13

## Decision

The one sentence a hiring manager should be able to say about Aju after 60 seconds on the homepage:

**"He's built products from zero and run the P&L to scale them."**

This is the positioning anchor. The homepage hero, value proposition, and featured case studies must reinforce this sentence. Everything else (AI expertise, international experience, team building) is supporting evidence, not the headline.

## Alternatives

1. **Lead with AI.** "He ships AI systems, not decks about AI." Differentiating but narrows the aperture. Excludes Layer 1 roles (VP Product, GM, Head of Growth) that are not AI specific. AI is the proof, not the headline.
2. **Lead with P&L scale.** "He's run $27M P&Ls and $260M processing platforms." Impressive but static. Does not communicate the builder dimension (0 to 1) that separates Aju from pure operators.
3. **Use the full 26 word UVP.** "Product commercial leader who combines 0 to 1 to N product incubation with full P&L ownership, GTM execution, and hands on AI deployment." Too long to remember. A hiring manager will compress it anyway; better to control the compression.

## Why

- The two rarest things in the profile are 0 to 1 incubation AND P&L ownership in the same person. Most operators do one or the other.
- This sentence maps directly to Layer 1 target roles (VP Product, GM, Head of Growth) which combine product ownership with commercial accountability.
- AI expertise is the "how," not the "what." Leading with AI narrows to Layer 2A roles (Head of AI, VP AI) which are stretch targets.

## Consequences

- The homepage hero must communicate "builds from zero" and "runs the P&L" within the first viewport.
- Case study ordering on the homepage should lead with Ensemble (0 to 1) and follow with one of the P&L scale stories.
- AI case studies are featured but positioned as proof of the builder identity, not as the primary identity.
- The exact copy will go through the Humanizer Gate before being finalized. This ADR captures the intent, not the final words.
