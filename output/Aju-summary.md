## WORK EXAMPLES TO SHOWCASE (Case Studies)

### Case Study 1: CanadaHelps Ensemble — 0 to 1 SaaS Incubation

**Outcome:** 200+ customers, $36K MRR ($432K ARR), NPS 57, 55% stickiness, 86% PLG acquired in under 18 months.

**The story:** Inherited four disconnected teams with no aligned strategy. Diagnosed gaps in first 90 days. Built one coherent operating strategy. Led an 8 person cross functional pod through customer discovery, ICP definition, pricing, packaging, roadmap, and launch. Made the hard call to sunset a legacy CRM with negative unit economics ($115 cost per $100 earned) and reallocated headcount into growth. Personally closed the first ~15 deals to validate the market before building the sales motion.

**Decision narrative:** Had to choose between optimizing the existing product line or betting resources on incubation. Chose incubation because customer discovery showed a segment (mid tier nonprofits) was underserved and willing to pay. Killed the CRM to fund it. Board approved the plan based on the business case.

### Case Study 2: AI Sales Agent — Deploying AI as a Commercial Operating Lever

**Outcome:** Lead conversion from 5% to 45% (45 percentage point lift).

**The story:** The sales team was manually qualifying leads with inconsistent criteria. Built a human in the loop AI agent that orchestrated web research, internal business rules, API calls, evidence backed scoring, and next best action recommendations. Human review before execution. The agent did the research; the rep made the judgment call.

**Decision narrative:** Could have hired more reps (the conventional answer). Instead bet on AI augmentation. The agent handles the repeatable research; humans handle relationship and judgment. Took ~3 months from concept to production.

### Case Study 3: HPCC Platform Commercialization — Internal Tech to $50M+ Business

**Outcome:** $50M+ in new revenue. 100+ enterprise clients. 25,000+ developer community.

**The story:** LexisNexis had a proprietary big data platform used only internally. Saw the commercial opportunity, built the business case, and got CEO and parent company chairman approval. Built the entire go to market from scratch: reseller network, delivery partnerships, marketplace, developer community for open adoption. Applied lean commercialization loops to refine product market fit.

**Decision narrative:** 12 business cases totaling $38M in investment requests. Had to convince both U.S. CEO and U.K. parent company (Reed Elsevier) executive chairman. Built the case on market sizing, competitive gaps, and channel economics.

### Case Study 4: SBA Covid Loan Deployment — $5B in Under 3 Months

**Outcome:** $5B in financing to 100,000+ small businesses.

**The story:** Government mandated that banks deploy SBA loan payment rails in weeks. Normal timeline: 45 to 60 days. Led the cross company initiative at Fiserv, coordinating across multiple product and engineering teams under extreme time pressure. Delivered in under 3 months.

**Decision narrative:** No room for normal product process. Triaged scope ruthlessly, parallel tracked workstreams, and shipped. The kind of execution story that shows what happens when constraints are real.

### Case Study 5: AI Competitive Intelligence System — Production AI Build

**Outcome:** Live product at competitive-analysis-ui.vercel.app.

**The story:** Built end to end: Next.js frontend on Vercel, serverless API layer, n8n agentic pipeline running parallel web searches, evidence shaping for LLM consumption, Gemini for synthesis, strict JSON schema validation before output reaches the user. No hallucinated comparisons get through. The schema gate rejects malformed output.

**Decision narrative:** Chose Gemini Flash over GPT 4o after comparing factual grounding and output consistency on structured tasks. Selected for latency and schema compliance. Designed retrieval, orchestration, reasoning, and validation as distinct layers for independent testing and iteration.

### Case Study 6: AI QuickBooks Reconciliation System — Client Consulting Engagement

**Outcome:** Reduced monthly reconciliation from a full week to under 45 minutes.

**The story:** CPA firm client whose QBO automation was unreliable, triggering manual review of every transaction. Solution: let QBO auto post, then run a month end AI auditor. QBO API integration (read only GL extraction), bank statement PDF parsing, reconciliation engine surfacing duplicates and miscategorizations for structured human review. Read only design earned accountant trust from day one.

**Decision narrative:** Trust first architecture. Read only against the GL eliminates risk of data corruption. Human corrections feed a self improving feedback loop. Solve the trust problem first, keep humans in the loop, build the feedback loop from day one.
