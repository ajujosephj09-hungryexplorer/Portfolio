# 08 — Contact page

**What to build:** The Contact page in Persuade mode. A visitor who arrives here is ready to reach out. The page eliminates all friction between intent and action. 30-second conversion target: a visitor should complete their action (send an email or connect on LinkedIn) within 30 seconds of landing.

One invitation line (one sentence, concise, not clever — no "let's build something together" copy). Primary contact: email (mailto:ajujosephj09@gmail.com) and LinkedIn profile link (https://www.linkedin.com/in/aju-joseph-john/), both prominent. Secondary links: Substack (https://ajujosephjohn.substack.com/) and GitHub (https://github.com/ajujosephj09-hungryexplorer). No contact form in V1 (mailto only, per PRD). No calendar booking widget, no map, no decorative content padding the page.

All contact links must be tappable with generous hit targets on mobile (recruiters review on phones between meetings). The page should feel like a business card, not a landing page.

Page-specific meta title, description, OG tags, canonical URL. Heading hierarchy: h1 = page title. All copy through Humanizer Gate and Post Humanizer Gate.
