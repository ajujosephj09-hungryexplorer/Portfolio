# 07 — Contact page

**What to build:** The contact page in Persuade mode. Intentionally minimal. The page exists to eliminate friction between intent and action. 30-second conversion target: a visitor who arrives with intent to reach out should complete that action in under 30 seconds.

One invitation line (one sentence, concise, not clever). Primary contact: email (mailto:ajujosephj09@gmail.com) and LinkedIn profile link, both prominent. Secondary links: Substack and GitHub. No contact form in V1 (mailto only). No calendar booking widget, no map, no decorative content padding.

All contact links must be tappable with generous hit targets on mobile (recruiters review on phones between meetings). Page-specific meta title, description, OG tags, canonical URL. Heading hierarchy: h1 = page title. All copy through Humanizer Gate and Post Humanizer Gate.
