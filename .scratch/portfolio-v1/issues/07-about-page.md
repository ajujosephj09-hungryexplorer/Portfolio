# 07 — About page

**What to build:** The About page in Read mode. A hiring manager who has read a case study or scanned the homepage comes here to answer: is the breadth deliberate or scattered? Does the trajectory make sense?

The page has four content blocks after the nav:

**Career narrative:** 3-4 thematic paragraphs organized around the positioning line ("built products from zero and run the P&L to scale them"). This is explicitly not chronological. The paragraphs should be organized by theme (e.g., building from zero, scaling what exists, deploying AI as an operating lever, cross-border execution) so the breadth reads as deliberate range, not generalism. Content sourced from the intake summary differentiators and resume-archetypes bullet text.

**Experience timeline:** A ruled vertical list (horizontal rules as dividers, consistent with the site-wide structural device). Eight entries from Cognizant through Databricks. Each entry shows: period, role, company, 1-2 proof points. Mapleview Partners appears as a continuous advisory practice (Nov 2021 to Aug 2023, paused for CanadaHelps Sep 2023, resumed Jul 2026). CanadaHelps named and framed with commercial scale in every mention (ADR 0008). The career gap (Nov 2021 to Aug 2023) reads as deliberate relocation + productive consulting, not unemployment.

**Education:** Duke MBA (Fuqua School of Business), B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering (First Class with Distinction), Pragmatic Institute Certified (Build).

**What drives me:** Closing section sourced from the mnookin two-pager. Filtered for public-appropriate content only. Excludes: comp ranges ($300K-$400K), work authorization details (Indian passport, Canadian PR), and in-office mandate opinions. Includes: building from zero, full-stack ownership, AI for commercial problems, autonomy, cross-functional teams, cross-border perspective.

No headshot on this page (headshot lives on the landing page only, per about surface brief anti-goals). No skills grid, tag cloud, horizontal timeline, or testimonial placeholders. Person schema markup. Page-specific meta title, description, OG tags, canonical URL. Heading hierarchy: h1 = page title, h2 = section headings. CTA at the bottom. All copy through Humanizer Gate and Post Humanizer Gate.
